Who we are

Exceptional service and quality products

When it comes to fencing solutions in Perth, we at Fencing Finesse are dedicated to providing you with exceptional service and quality products.

Our dedicated team will listen carefully to your needs and then put together a fencing solution that suits you. We cover anything ranging from fence installation to manufacturing, for residential and commercial clients.

Our products

In addition to providing a fence installation service, we also manufacture a range of batten fencing and batten gates in Perth. We also specialise in open style pool fencing and perforated metal gates. If you have any specific requirements, contact us today.

Since 1992

We have been providing a fence installation and manufacturing service in Perth since 1992. During this time we have consistently delivered the highest standard of customer service and workmanship. We stand by the quality of our work and are proud of what we have achieved.
